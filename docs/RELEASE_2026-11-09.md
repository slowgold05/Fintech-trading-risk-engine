## What the merged source fixed

Four findings from the 9 November review:

1. Portfolio performance uses its own `1y` / `2y` allowlist. Other chart range tokens are not accepted on that contract.
2. Zero-capital periods return null percentages. A defined terminal loss of 100% is retained when that is the actual result. Known dollar amounts stay populated. Empty accounts are not rewritten as a fabricated zero return.
3. Risk metrics are composed on one clock and one snapshot, including the internal reads. An outer wrapper around readers that roll themselves back is not the fix that was accepted.
4. Pending-job admission is atomic per owner: research cap 10, forecast cap 5, taken under an owner lock.

Two further release blockers, found after that review:

- A worker claim is not released by the helper's commit or rollback. Release uses a separate source session. A test that shares the helper's connection does not prove that isolation. The proof that counts uses an independent connection on PostgreSQL.
- The current application role has grants on the six research and forecast tables and on the SEC filings cache, limited to the minimal columns those ORM inserts use, including nullable INSERT fields. `users.role` changes and ledger rewrites stay forbidden.

Optional research synthesis and Kronos inference stay off. No real Kronos weight inference and no forecast-accuracy claim are part of this release.
